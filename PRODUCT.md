# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, statically exported for deployment to Cloudflare Pages. Styling is implemented locally without a component framework. The stack was selected to preserve strong pre-rendered SEO while leaving a straightforward path to server routes and a database if the product grows.

## Users

Landlords, letting agents, homeowners, offices, commercial premises, and property managers in Preston and across England who need cleaning or property compliance services coordinated reliably.

## Product Purpose

Coreserve Facilities Group presents its services, explains how work is coordinated, and converts qualified visitors into phone calls or quote enquiries. Success means visitors quickly understand the available services, coverage, and next step.

## Positioning

One practical point of contact for cleaning and common property compliance arrangements, with clear scope, scheduling, and communication from enquiry through completion.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive while arranging cleaning, an EPC assessment, a landlord gas safety check, or an electrical safety inspection. They need to identify the appropriate service, confirm likely coverage, and provide property and timescale details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three public routes at launch: Home, Services, and Contact.
- Four core services: cleaning, EPC assessments, gas safety coordination, and electrical safety/EICR coordination.
- Preserve all relevant content from the supplied `index.html`, excluding the final two service cards.
- The quote experience must work without a database and remain easy to connect to an email provider or database later.
- The site must be responsive, accessible, search-engine friendly, and ready for Cloudflare's free deployment tier.
- Regulated work must be described as completed or coordinated through appropriately qualified professionals; the site must not fabricate accreditations or certification held directly by Coreserve.

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: Coreserve Facilities Group.
- Preserve the established navy, teal, pale blue-grey, and white palette from `index.html`.
- Voice: professional, practical, straightforward, dependable, and free of inflated claims.
- Current phone: 07728 344412.
- Current address: 45 Symonds Road, Fulwood, Preston, PR2 3DH.
- Preston-based with availability across England to be confirmed per enquiry.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied `index.html` contains the confirmed business copy, service categories, contact details, service area, process, and visual identity. No testimonials, customer logos, case studies, trade memberships, verified business email address, or formal accreditations have been supplied; future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make the next step clear without pressuring the visitor.
- Explain regulated services accurately and avoid implying unverified credentials.
- Use service-specific pages and metadata to answer real search intent.
- Keep the production setup inexpensive and understandable.
- Prefer fast, durable pages over unnecessary application complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus states, reduced-motion support, descriptive labels, readable contrast, and responsive layouts suitable for mobile and desktop visitors.
